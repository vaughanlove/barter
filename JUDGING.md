# Hackathon Judging

Regenerated from the official rules and rubric for the Meta AI Developer
Hackathon. Submission deadline: November 4, 9:00 PM EST, via Cerebral Valley.
A three minute demo video is required and is a primary judging artifact,
alongside the code and the live product.

## Rules (pass/fail before scoring)

- Open source: repository must be public.
- Team size: maximum four participants. Solo is allowed.
- Demo must highlight only features, code, and functionality built during
  the Hackathon. Judges must be able to clearly identify original
  contributions. Failure to identify them risks disqualification.
- New work only: what is presented must have been built during the event.
  Building on existing products is allowed if newly developed work is
  clearly identified.
- Disqualified: projects that violate legal, ethical, or platform
  policies, or use code, data, or assets without rights.
- Anti-patterns named by organizers: medical advice AI, basic RAG
  applications, simple image analyzers, simple AI-for-education chatbots,
  AI job application screeners, AI nutrition coaches, personality
  analyzers, sports analyzers/coaches, anything political or involving war.

## Judging process

### Stage One (pass/fail)

Baseline viability: the project reasonably fits the theme and reasonably
used the Meta Model API and/or Meta's open-weight models to build the
project during the Hackathon.

### Stage Two (scored 0-10 per criterion)

| Criterion | Weight | What earns a high score |
|---|---|---|
| Impact | 30% | Real-world potential; who benefits and how much; whether it could become something people use; fit to a problem statement |
| Use of Meta AI Tooling | 25% | Creative use of Meta's AI tooling; beyond a basic integration; unexpected capabilities surfaced |
| Technical Depth and Execution | 20% | Sound, thoughtfully refined engineering; real craft |
| Demo | 25% | Working, impressive demo; product holds up live; quality of the demo video |

### Stage Three

Top 5 projects per track advance. Final judges watch the video demos live
and rank using the same rubric.

## Tracks and prizes

Two tracks: Indie Developer ($400k pool) and Startups ($550k pool), plus
bonus prizes. Total pool $1,000,000.
